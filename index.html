About

From code, to design, to both.

D

I started out writing code. At a tech academy I learned front-end web development, and I could make things work. But I kept noticing that my projects didn't look or feel as thoughtful as the products I admired.

That gap led me to product design, and I fell in love with it. I taught myself UI/UX through YouTube, online courses and a lot of practice, and I've spent the last 3+ years designing websites, web apps and mobile apps for companies and founders.

Because I came from code, I design with the build in mind: interfaces that are clear for users, realistic for developers, and ready to ship. When it helps, I build the front-end too.

I'm especially drawn to products that make people's lives genuinely better. I'm open to freelance projects and full-time roles, remote or in Lagos.
