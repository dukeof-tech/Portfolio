Village Hub: one building, every service, around the clock.

The website for a 24-hour student and community hub beside the University of Ibadan, bringing co-working, laundry, food and events into one place people can use from their phones.

The brief

Village Hub puts very different services under one roof: co-working, laundry, food and events. Its audience is students who "live on their phones and need everything in one place".

The website had to explain all of that at a glance, feel like a place you'd want to spend time in, and get people booking.

What I did

  • Designed and built the website: the homepage and the Spaces, Laundry, Food and Events sections
  • Booking and membership flows, so the main actions are always one tap away
  • An installable web app, so regulars can keep Village Hub on their home screen

What it does

01

Spaces

Hot desks, quiet zones and meeting rooms, booked by the hour or with a full-day pass.

02

Laundry

Book a machine from anywhere, pay from your phone, and get a notification when it's done.

03

Food & ordering

Order from several vendors at once and pay once.

04

Membership

Easy check-in, discounted pricing plans and merch for members.
