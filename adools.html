Adools: a website for a studio that builds.

The company website for Adools Construct & Designs, an architecture, interior design and construction studio working in Nigeria and Ghana since 2016.

adoolsdesign.com
My role
Design & front-end development
Company
Adools Construct & Designs (in-house)
Timeline
2025
Status
Live

The brief

Adools does a lot: architecture, interior design, building construction, turnkey and modular builds, 3D visualisation and project management. It also runs its own ventures, including FewSure.

The site needed to show all of that, feel as considered as the studio's buildings, and still make it easy for a client to get in touch.

What I did

  • Designed and built the website
  • Structured the content into four clear areas, Projects, Services, Studio and Ventures, so each kind of visitor finds their path quickly
  • Made contact easy with a "Contact us" button in the navigation and a quick WhatsApp button, which is how most clients here prefer to reach a business

Design choices

  • Lead with real homes and real people, not floor plans: the hero video shows what the studio's work feels like to live in
  • A calm, dark palette with one gold accent, to feel premium without getting in the way of the photography
  • Few words, big type. "Rooted in Space. Refined by Purpose." does the work of a paragraph
