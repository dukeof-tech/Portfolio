FewSure: build your house, small small.

A platform that helps Nigerians plan, save towards and build their own home step by step, instead of waiting until they have all the money.

fewsure.com
My role
Product manager, lead product designer & front-end developer
Company
Adools Construct & Designs
Timeline
2025 – present
Status
Website live with a waitlist · Mobile app in development

The problem

For most people in Nigeria, owning a home means having a large amount of money up front. Very few people have that at once, so the dream keeps getting pushed back.

FewSure starts from a different idea: begin with what you have today, make a plan, and keep going until the house is built.

What I did

  • Product: shaped what FewSure does first and how the website and app work together, as the product manager
  • Design: designed the website and the mobile app, from the user flows to the final screens
  • Build: built the website front end myself, so the design shipped as intended

What it does

01

Cost calculator

Estimates what a house might cost based on the house type, location and materials, and says clearly that it's an estimate, not a quote.

02

Easy Build

Breaks building work into smaller steps that you plan and pay for one at a time.

03

Premium Build

For people who want the whole project planned from start to finish.

04

Savings & progress

One view of your building plan, your savings progress and your balance.

Design choices

  • Talk like the user. "Pay small small" is how people describe saving towards something big. Using it makes the product feel made for them, not translated for them.
  • Give value before asking for anything. The cost calculator answers the first real question, "how much will my house cost?", before asking anyone to sign up.
  • Be honest about money. Estimates are labelled as estimates, and the site says plainly which services are still being built.
  • One clear next step. Every section leads back to a single action: join the waitlist.

Where it stands

The website is live and collecting a waitlist, and the mobile app is being designed and built now. I'll add launch results to this page as they come in.
