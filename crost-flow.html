Crost Flow: credit, made simple for Nigerians.

A credit card service with a mobile app and website, designed around how young Nigerians and freelancers actually spend, save and borrow.

My role
Product & UI design, research
Team
Brand by Agirgbe-Adeeko Ayomide
Tools
Figma, Jitter
Deliverables
Mobile app, website (light + dark)

The challenge

Convenient, user-friendly credit has long been hard to get in Nigeria. Traditional banks are slow and paperwork-heavy, and many digital tools aren't designed around how Nigerians actually spend and save.

That leaves a real gap for people who want a simple, trustworthy way to manage everyday spending and build financial stability. Freelancers and young people are hit hardest.

Research

I reviewed competing fintech and credit products to find where they lose people. Three themes kept coming up:

  • Confusing onboarding: long forms and slow approvals
  • Unclear fees and terms that make people distrust the product
  • Generic rewards that don't match local spending

What I designed

  • The mobile app: account overview, spending insights with clear charts, and simple card controls
  • The website in light and dark versions: the product story, key benefits (security, flexible repayment, digital control), FAQs and a clear "Apply" call to action
  • Build planning: I worked with a developer on a React/Expo build plan

The solution

The core idea: "Finance Made Easier."

01

Simple onboarding

A mobile-first application with less paperwork and faster approval.

02

A clear digital experience

An easy app for managing the card, tracking spending and redeeming rewards.

03

Rewards that fit

Discounts on local services and cashback on everyday essentials.

04

Transparent terms

Plain-language terms that build trust and financial literacy.

What I'd do differently today

I'd pair the competitor review with direct conversations with freelancers and young Nigerians, and test the onboarding flow with real users before finalising. Research tells you where others fail; talking to users tells you what your product needs to get right.
